How these numbers are made. Leave-one-out drops one live token, re-estimates every category and global median from the other 127, and predicts the dropped token — the anchor never prices itself. Walk-forward trains on the oldest 60% of dated listings and tests on the newest 40%, the sets strictly isolated. Top-20 recall compares the full model's predicted top 20 against the market's actual top 20. All three are out-of-sample; the in-sample Spearman (~0.53) is not used as a validation number.

How this board is built, in four rules:
  1. Zero noise: the board lists all 196 rankable pre-token protocols — the 40 watchlist entries plus every one of the 156 early finds the snapshot holds. Nothing rankable is left out; nothing unrankable is let in.
  2. Strict token isolation: a protocol counts as pre-token only when the snapshot records no market cap, no Gecko ID and no symbol. Anything with a traded token is physically isolated from the ranking board and shown only in the Live tokens view.
  3. Fundamental valuation: implied values come purely from peer category medians and snapshot-native metrics, capped by a logarithmic soft cap so one giant TVL cannot hallucinate a ten-billion-dollar token.
  4. Domain-aware exclusions: liquidity-routing artifacts — bridges, canonical bridges, cross-chain bridges, liquid staking and restaking, restaked BTC, CEX listings and stablecoin wrappers — are blacklisted by category, and versioned, wrapped and exchange-prefixed names are excluded by rule.

How the radar prices a token that does not exist yet

Every number comes from the sealed snapshot: the protocol's TVL, its seven-day growth, how long it has run with no token, and how many chains it runs on. No name is ever special-cased.

raw      = TVL × multiple × growth × maturity × breadth
implied  = softCap(raw, $500M) = $500M + $500M × ln(1 + (raw − $500M) / $500M) for raw > $500M
multiple = shrunk median mcap/TVL of the category's live tokens
growth   = 1 + clamp(7d change, −50%, +100%) × 0.4
maturity = 0.7 + min(age, 1095 days) / 1095 × 0.5
breadth  = 1 + min(chains − 1, 9) × 0.01

The multiple is the market's own verdict: for each category, the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of its live tokens, winsorized at 5× and shrunk toward the whole market's median with a pseudo-count of 8, then clamped to [0.05, 1.5]. A category with no live token prices at the market median.

Validation methodology

Three out-of-sample tests, all computed at build time from the snapshot's 128 live-token anchors and shown on the Model tab: leave-one-out (each anchor predicted from the other 127, medians re-estimated per fold), walk-forward (the oldest 60% of dated listings price the newest 40%), and top-20 recall (the full model's predicted top 20 against the market's actual top 20). The page re-derives the Spearman benchmark in the browser from the injected anchors and lights the integrity strip only when it matches the build-time value exactly. Nothing here is an in-sample figure dressed up as validation.

Exclusion rules

Protocols in the categories Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Cross Chain Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking, Restaked BTC, CEX and Stablecoin Wrapper are blacklisted. Names ending in a version number, names containing the whole words wrapped, staked or staking, and names starting with an exchange name are excluded. The snapshot carries no parent-protocol field, so sub-products cannot be identified from it — no heuristic rule is invented for them, and the ranking stays purely data-driven.

Farm verdict methodology

The costed verdict prices one farming wallet: the TGE pool is 5% of the damped implied value, split among an estimated farmer count of 10,000 + sqrt(TVL) × a chain sybil factor (Solana and Arbitrum 5.0, Ethereum 0.5, Bitcoin 0.1, unlisted chains 1.0). Costs are chain gas per farm step plus the opportunity cost of 180 days' locked capital at 5%, on a capital ladder of $500 to $5,000 by TVL. A row earns FARM only when the net clears $100 with ROI above 1.0; a positive net below that bar is THIN; a negative net is SKIP.

Token odds methodology

The launch probability is the category's Bayesian-smoothed launch rate, (live + 2) / (total + 5), times an age factor (0.5 past 1,500 days, 1.2 in the 180–730 day window, 1.0 otherwise and for unknown age), clamped to [1%, 99%]. Odds are a display badge only: the ranking is by implied value and nothing else.

Each row carries six steps built from its own record: the category sets the action, the first chain sets the gas cost, TVL sets the capital, age sets the hold.
